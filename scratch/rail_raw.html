Product Rail Gallery – Fashion Store Hero Section

Heroes• 2K

Preview
About

Product Rail Gallery, a fashion store hero section for React and Tailwind CSS (shadcn/ui): real garment photos hang from wooden hangers on a clothing rail (a clothing rack, in American English), turn to face the shopper on hover, slide along the rail with hanger physics, and lift off the rail to show the back. It is made for e-commerce: online clothing stores, fashion and apparel brands, streetwear drops and lookbooks that want a shop hero and product showcase shoppers can handle like a rack in a store. Each piece takes a front, back and side photo (transparent PNG or WebP) or falls back to built-in illustrations for tees, knits, hoodies, shirts, jackets and coats. The rail is a one-dimensional physics simulation: dragging a hanger pushes its neighbours, released pieces swing and settle, and the hovered piece turns with a perspective rotation. Keyboard users get a roving tabindex with arrow keys, Home/End and Enter; touch users tap to turn and tap again to lift; the detail view is an accessible modal with Escape, focus trapping and focus return. Autoplay, interval, hanger style, labels and an onOpenChange callback are props. Light and dark mode, prefers-reduced-motion, no dependencies beyond React.

Built with React and Tailwind. Copy the prompt and paste it into Claude Code, Cursor or Lovable: your agent adds Product Rail Gallery – Fashion Store Hero Section to your project and fits it to your theme.
